FIELD NOTES

Game guide

This guide describes this early approximation. The game pauses while it is open.

Power-ups

Walk over a star-shaped upgrade to collect it; its label names the effect. Upgrades can appear in treasure rooms, shops, challenge chests, and after bosses. Shop upgrades cost coins. Their effects last for the rest of the run.

Damage · passive
Adds 1.5 damage to each tear. Repeated pickups add more damage.
Rate · passive
Reduces the delay between volleys by 0.045 seconds, to a minimum of 0.12 seconds. Repeated pickups stack until that limit.
Speed · passive
Adds 25 movement speed. Repeated pickups stack.
Range · passive
Adds 100 units to the maximum distance tears can travel; walls and rocks can stop them sooner unless another upgrade changes that. Repeated pickups stack.
Health · passive
Adds and fills up to one red-heart container, subject to the 12-heart limit. Repeated pickups can add more containers until that limit.
Double shot (DOUBLE) · passive
Each volley fires two angled tears; each uses your current tear damage.
Piercing tears (PIERCE) · passive
Tears pass through enemies and can hit each enemy once. Rocks still stop them unless you also have Spectral tears.
Splash tears (BURST) · passive
When a tear hits an enemy, other exposed enemies nearby take half that tear’s damage. Underground burrowers are not affected.
Spectral tears (SPECTRAL) · passive
Tears pass through rocks. They still stop at walls and enemies unless another upgrade changes that.
Rebound tears (BOUNCE) · passive
Each tear can bounce off room walls twice before disappearing. Its normal range still limits its lifetime.
Returning tears (RETURN) · passive
Halfway through its lifetime, each surviving tear reverses direction once. Homing stops steering it after the reversal. Without piercing, a hit still destroys it.
Patchwork Heart · active
Press Space when all three bars are filled to restore up to one red heart. Clearing a combat or boss room adds one bar, up to three. Using it at full red health still spends the charge.
Fury Tome · active
Press Space with three charges to add 2 tear damage until you leave the room. Multiple uses in the same room stack. It combines with tear modifiers.
Ashen Bell · active
Press Space with four charges to deal 25 damage to each exposed enemy in the room. Underground burrowers are unaffected.
Blood Power (BLOOD) · bargain
Adds 3 damage to each tear; repeated purchases add more.
Homing tears (HOMING) · bargain
Tears gently turn toward a nearby exposed enemy. Homing combines with double shot, piercing, splash, and damage upgrades; another homing pickup has no extra effect.
Lantern · unlocked start
After your first victory, choose the Lantern before starting a run to begin with one soul heart and 25 extra movement speed. The soul heart can be lost to damage.

Spectral, rebound, and returning tears combine with double shot, piercing, splash, and homing. Each tear can hit an enemy only once even if it returns or bounces. Repeated copies of these three modifiers have no extra effect. Damage, rate, speed, range, and health pickups stack within their limits. Double shot, piercing, and splash combine with each other and with damage and rate upgrades. Taking a second copy of double shot, piercing, splash, or homing has no additional effect.

Active items

Each treasure room also contains a fully charged Fury Tome or Ashen Bell. Walk over its diamond to swap it with your held active item; the old one stays on the floor with its current charge. Only one active item can be held. Step away briefly before swapping back. Combat and boss room clears add one charge to the held item; the challenge room adds one after all waves. A battery pickup adds one charge to the held item; it is left on the ground if the item is full. Shops sell batteries for five coins, and cleared rooms can drop them. The current item and charge appear in the upper-right HUD. These items and values are original approximations of Rebirth active-item mechanics.

Curse room

Each floor has an optional purple-marked curse room with two red chests. Crossing its door in either direction deals half a heart of damage unless you are currently invulnerable. Protective hearts are lost before red health; losing red health reduces the chance of a Bargain portal. Each chest randomly gives two soul hearts, a bomb and a key, three coins, or an ambush of two enemies. An ambush seals the room until defeated and charges Patchwork Heart on clearance. Chest odds and contents, guaranteed room generation, and door behavior are simplified approximations of Rebirth.

Challenge room

Each floor has an optional orange-marked challenge room. Its chest shows a passive upgrade. Touch it to take the upgrade and begin three waves of enemies; the doors stay shut until all three waves are defeated. Clearing all waves adds one charge to Patchwork Heart. You can leave without opening the chest. The room is an approximation: it always appears, has no health-entry requirement, and uses ordinary enemies rather than Rebirth’s full challenge-room rules.

The Bargain

After defeating the boss on floors 2–5, a diamond-marked portal may appear. It is guaranteed if you took no red-heart damage on that floor; otherwise its chance is 25%. Soul and black hearts absorb damage before red hearts. This is a simplified approximation of Rebirth’s deal rooms.

In the bargain room, walk over a star to trade the displayed red-heart containers for its upgrade. Blood Power costs one container; Homing costs two. Containers and any red health that no longer fits are lost for the run. The game refuses a purchase that would leave you with no health; soul and black hearts can keep you alive, but cannot be spent as payment. Use the upward-marked portal to return to the boss room. You can leave without buying.

Cards & pills

Walk over a card or pill to pick it up. You can hold one at a time; picking up another swaps it with the one on the floor. Press Q to use the held card or pill once. Its name appears when picked up and in the upper-right HUD.

Card and pill drops and their effects are chosen at random. This game shows an effect's name before use; it does not have unidentified pills.

The Fool · card
Returns you to the starting room of the current floor.
The Lovers · card
Places two full red-heart pickups on the ground.
Death · card
Deals 40 damage to each exposed enemy in the current room; underground burrowers are unaffected.
Full Health · pill
Fills your red-heart containers. It does not add protective hearts.
Health Up · pill
Adds one full red-heart container and fills it, if there is room within the health limit.
Health Down · pill
Removes up to one full red-heart container, keeping at least one if you already have one. It does not create a container if you have none. Current red health is reduced if needed.
Speed Up · pill
Increases movement speed for the rest of the run.

Deeper floors

Each chapter has two floors: Basement, Caves, then Depths. Depths II ends with an original final boss. Defeat it and enter the exit to win. Your first victory unlocks an optional Lantern start for future runs: one soul heart and faster movement. The unlock is saved in your browser when storage is available; if saving fails, it lasts only until this page is closed or reloaded. Caves introduce burrowers: they move as a dark patch while underground, cannot hurt you or be damaged then, and emerge to attack. Caves and Depths can also contain orange chargers: they pause, aim at you, then rush in a straight line. Depths add purple enemies that fire a three-shot spread. Each chapter has its own boss and attack pattern.

Hearts

Damage is measured in half hearts. Protective hearts are lost from right to left before red health. Containers plus protective hearts are limited to 12 full hearts.

♥ Red hearts
Your ordinary health fills red-heart containers. Red-heart pickups and the charged Patchwork Heart item restore missing red health, up to your container limit.
♥ Soul hearts
Blue protective health absorbs damage before red hearts. It is not restored by red-heart pickups.
♥ Black hearts
Protective health that absorbs damage before red hearts. When a full black heart is completely depleted, it deals 40 damage to each exposed enemy in the room; underground burrowers are unaffected.

Press Space to use the equipped active item when fully charged; clearing rooms recharges it. Press P to pause the game outside this guide.
