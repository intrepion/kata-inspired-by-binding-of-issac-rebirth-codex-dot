# Game vocabulary

- **Run**: one attempt, ending when the player runs out of health.
- **Floor**: a connected set of rooms with a start, treasure room, and boss room.
- **Room clear**: the point when every enemy in a room is defeated, unlocking its exits and charging the active item.
- **Active item**: a reusable effect charged by clearing rooms.
- **Pickup**: a resource or upgrade collected by touching it.
